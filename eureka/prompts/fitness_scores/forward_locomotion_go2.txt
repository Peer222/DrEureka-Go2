The task/curriculum score is computed as follows (averaged over all environments; summed over an episode):  
```python
def compute_task_score(self):
        target_velocity = ... # INSERT TARGET HERE # curriculum target in m/s
        lin_vel_error = torch.square(target_velocity - self.env.root_states[:, 7])
        return torch.exp(-lin_vel_error / 0.25)
```  
The maximum task/curriculum score that could be reached theoretically is 1000. In practice, scores above 700 are reasonable.  
The target velocity is updated according to the upcoming curriculum stage.
